This targeted primary-source check does not change the protocol, inputs, acceptance rules or paid requests. It supplements the earlier product decision frame; it is not an exhaustive literature review or a competitor evaluation.

These are vendor descriptions of capabilities, not measured results on our cases. We have not run these competing systems, established equal-compute comparisons with them, or established their Hindi/Telugu accuracy. Missing documentation would not establish that a capability is absent.

The credible near-term output is a reproducible failure analysis and, only if measured, a bounded improvement with fixed Sarvam models. Product differentiation requires a fresh, independently reviewed comparison showing that our method prevents meaningful user-facing errors better than ordinary source scoping, typed records and existing frameworks at an acceptable cost and latency. A benchmark contribution also requires dataset quality, public artifacts, clear denominators and an honest account of development exposure; it does not require pretending that the current intervention succeeded.

The present test can discriminate between these specific evidence-presentation choices. It cannot establish a missing persistent-memory layer because the relevant original evidence is already supplied. The final product decision must follow the complete results and their regressions.
