## Product interpretation

A gain would support a tested reporting-boundary improvement under these fixed models and synthetic development workflows. It would not prove long-term memory is necessary, that the original AgentMem English architecture is multilingual, that Sarvam lacks ordinary storage/approval tools, or that grouping/provenance is novel. A negative result rejects these interventions on this bank; it does not identify the underlying cause. Stronger ordinary controls, fresh tasks, independent language review, broader languages, action-level and cost/latency validation are required before a unique-product claim. This phase should decide whether there is enough measured benefit to warrant that work.
