## First workday: one defensible packing design

- Read the21-case source review. Separate interpretation of a source from sufficiency for the entire answer; aggregation still requires other records.
- Specify one general contract for original source spans and necessary context: immutable source IDs, offsets/hashes, role/date, entity/referent links, negation, modality and conditions; visible omissions; refuse compression when links cannot be resolved. Preserve assistant-authored evidence when the question depends on it. Do not select by gold answers or case IDs.

Same models and budgets. Keep broad ranking/vocabulary/time-contract changes outside this ablation.
